How email verification works
  • New users get a verification email automatically when they register.
  • Unverified users can log in but see an amber banner and have limited features.
  • Resend triggers a fresh link (48h expiry, single-use).
  • Verify marks the email verified immediately (useful when SMTP isn't live yet).
